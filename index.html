Skip bin sizes for Mackay jobs

Mackay jobs range from storm and garden clean-ups to Queenslander renovations and new builds in growing suburbs like Rural View and Ooralea. What can and can't go in a skip

Waste type matters as much as size. Operators tip loads at licensed facilities such as council's Paget Waste Management Centre, and each type of waste costs a different amount to dispose of.

Usually accepted

  • General household waste: furniture, toys, clothes, boxes, cupboard contents and general junk.
  • Green waste: palm fronds, tropical prunings, lawn clippings and storm-damaged branches (often cheaper on its own, with no dirt or rubbish mixed in).
  • Renovation waste: timber, plasterboard, old cabinets, carpet, tiles and fittings.
  • Heavy or clean fill: soil, sand, concrete, bricks, rock and pavers (often needs its own bin and has a lower weight limit).
  • Scrap metal: old roofing iron, fencing, farm gates and machinery parts.

Not accepted, or extra charge

  • Asbestos and fibro: never in a normal skip (see below).
  • Tyres: usually banned or charged per tyre.
  • Car and household batteries: a fire risk in a skip.
  • Gas bottles and aerosols: can explode when compacted.
  • Paint, oils, chemicals and liquids: including pool chemicals, farm chemicals, sump oil and paint.
  • Mattresses: often accepted for an extra fee.
  • Food waste, medical waste and e-waste: check first. Many operators won't take TVs, computers or fluorescent tubes.

A note on asbestos

Many older Mackay homes, especially Queenslanders and post-war houses, have fibro walls, eaves or sheds that may contain asbestos. In Queensland, the state's asbestos guidance says a homeowner can remove up to 10 m² of non-friable (bonded) asbestos without a licence. More than that needs a Queensland Health-approved homeowner's certificate or a licensed removalist, and friable asbestos must be removed by a Class A licensed removalist. See asbestos.qld.gov.au. If you suspect asbestos, say so on the quote form. Never hide it in a general skip.

Dropping things off yourself

Mackay Regional Council's Paget Waste Management Centre is at 42 Crichtons Road, Paget, off Connors Road. Council lists the transfer station as open 6:15am to 5:45pm daily. Domestic asbestos loads of 175 kg or less are accepted if double-wrapped and clearly labelled; staff check the packaging and you unload it yourself. Council also runs smaller transfer stations around the region. Check the Mackay Regional Council website for hours, fees and accepted items.

Putting a skip on the street: Mackay Regional Council rules

Last updated: 24 October 2026

On your own property

Your own driveway, yard or a firm patch of ground is the easiest place for a skip. Council's approval is for skips on a road or footway, so a bin wholly on your property generally doesn't need it. After heavy rain, lawns get soft, so a driveway or hardstand is safer for the truck.

On the road, footpath or nature strip

Mackay Regional Council requires an "Approval for works on public areas and roads" to place a skip on a road or footway, under its Subordinate Local Laws 1.1 and 1.16. Council's 2025–26 fees list $369 for the "Alteration/improvements: shipping containers, rubbish skips etc." category. Fees change each financial year, so confirm the current amount.

Before you book a skip on the street, check with Mackay Regional Council on 1300 622 529 or council@mackay.qld.gov.au, or visit the council website. Mention it on the quote form too, because the operator may be able to advise.

Wherever the skip goes, keep it clear of driveways, hydrants, bus stops and footpaths, and don't fill it above the rim.
