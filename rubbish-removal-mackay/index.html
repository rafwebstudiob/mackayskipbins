Rubbish Removal Mackay: Skip Bins vs Pick-up

In short: for a big clean-up, a skip bin is often the easier way to remove household or green waste in Mackay. For small amounts, Mackay Regional Council's Paget tip and your waste vouchers are worth knowing about.

The main ways to get rid of household and garden rubbish in Mackay, what each one takes, and when a skip bin makes more sense.

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Your options for rubbish removal in Mackay

In short: For a big clean-up, a skip bin is usually the simplest way to remove household or garden waste. For a load or two, the Paget Waste Management Centre and your council waste vouchers may be enough.

Most Mackay households have two ways to get rid of more rubbish than fits in their bins:

  1. A skip bin, delivered to your driveway and taken away when you're done.
  2. Taking it to the tip yourself, at Mackay Regional Council's Paget Waste Management Centre, using your waste vouchers.

Which one makes sense depends on how much you have, what it is, and whether you have a trailer and the time to do the runs.

Option 1: a skip bin

In short: A skip suits jobs with a lot of mixed rubbish, heavy waste or many trailer loads of garden waste, because you load it at your own pace and it's taken away in one go.

A skip sits on your driveway (or on the street with council approval) for an agreed hire period. You can fill it over a few days, and you don't need a trailer, a ute or anyone to lift heavy items. We couldn't find a council kerbside bulky waste pick-up for Mackay, so for furniture, garden waste, soil, bricks or renovation waste, a skip saves the tip runs, as long as you choose the right bin and tell the operator. They can't take asbestos, gas bottles, batteries, tyres or chemicals in a normal load. See what can go in a skip and the size guide.

For a deceased estate, a house move or a big shed or garage job, see our clean-outs guide.

Option 2: the Paget tip and your waste vouchers

In short: If you have a trailer and only a load or two, you can take rubbish to council's Paget Waste Management Centre yourself, and your waste vouchers can cover some of it.

Mackay Regional Council's Paget Waste Management Centre is on Crichtons Road, Paget. Council lists the transfer station as open Monday to Sunday, 6:15am to 5:45pm, and it takes domestic and commercial waste. The RefindIt resource recovery drop-off there is open 7am to 5pm daily (closed Christmas Day and Good Friday), and the RefindIt by Outlook reuse shop sits next to the entry. Council's rural transfer stations, such as Sarina, Seaforth and Otterburn, take domestic waste only, and Bucasia and Walkerston take green waste only, each on limited days.

Residential property owners receive three waste vouchers (formerly dump vouchers) per rates notice, and landlords should pass them on to tenants. Vouchers cover self-hauled green waste or non-recyclable household waste, under one tonne per visit. For green waste, one voucher covers a car, ute, van or trailer. For household waste, one voucher covers a sedan or wagon, and two cover a ute, van or trailer. They can't be used for tyres, car bodies, asbestos, regulated or commercial waste, recyclables must be removed first, and vouchers expire at the end of the six-month rating period.

Does Mackay have a kerbside bulky waste pick-up?

In short: We couldn't find one on council's waste pages. Council directs bulky household items to its waste facilities.

Mackay Regional Council's waste information covers bin collections, its waste facilities and waste vouchers, and we couldn't find a kerbside bulky waste or hard rubbish collection for residents. Bulky items that won't fit in your bin can be taken to the Paget Waste Management Centre or another council facility. If that changes, check council's Rubbish and Bins pages.

What about my kerbside bins?

In short: Your bins are for everyday rubbish and recycling, not clean-ups.

Mackay Regional Council collects a general waste bin weekly and a yellow-lid recycling bin fortnightly, on the same day as your general waste. We couldn't find a separate council garden waste bin service, so larger amounts of garden waste can go to a council transfer station or green waste facility.

So which is easier?

In short: Pick a skip for big, mixed or heavy jobs. Use the tip and your vouchers for a load or two.

  • A skip bin usually makes sense for a full clean-out, a renovation, a big garden or shed job, heavy waste, or when you don't have a trailer or the time for several tip runs.
  • The Paget Waste Management Centre suits one or two trailer loads, especially while you still have vouchers.
  • The RefindIt reuse drop-off at Paget suits furniture and household items that are still in good condition.

Frequently asked questions

What is the easiest way to get rid of a lot of rubbish in Mackay?

It depends on how much you have and what it is. For one or two loads, a trailer run to the Paget Waste Management Centre, using your council waste vouchers if you have them, may be enough. For a big clean-out, heavy waste or many loads, a skip bin is often the easier option, because you load it at your own pace and it's taken away in one trip.

Where is the Mackay tip and when is it open?

Mackay Regional Council's Paget Waste Management Centre is on Crichtons Road, Paget. Council lists the transfer station as open Monday to Sunday, from 6:15am to 5:45pm.

How do Mackay waste vouchers work?

Residential property owners receive three waste vouchers (formerly dump vouchers) per rates notice, and landlords should pass them on to tenants. One voucher covers a car, ute or trailer of green waste. Household waste takes one voucher for a sedan or wagon and two for a ute, van or trailer, under one tonne per visit.

Does Mackay Regional Council pick up bulky rubbish?

We couldn't find a kerbside bulky waste collection for Mackay residents on council's website. Bulky items can be taken to the Paget Waste Management Centre or another council facility.

Can I use Mackay waste vouchers for a skip bin?

No. Council says vouchers are for self-hauled waste at its transfer stations and can't be redeemed for cash or used for other council services.

Can a skip bin take garden waste and building rubbish?

Usually, yes, which is one reason people choose a skip. Heavy waste such as soil, concrete and bricks normally needs a smaller bin with a weight limit, and asbestos can never go in a general skip. Tell the operator what you have when you request a quote.

Do I need a permit for a skip bin on the street in Mackay?

A skip wholly on your own property generally doesn't need council approval. A skip on the road, footpath or verge usually needs approval from Mackay Regional Council. See our council skip permits guide for details.
